About this portal

Parameter is a competition entry for the TSA Webmaster event, 2026-2027. The assignment: an interactive AI learning portal for high school students with at least three learning sections and a gamified, progress-tracking interface.

How the theme maps to the site

  • Fundamental AI concepts: Module 01, Foundations.
  • Practical AI tools and techniques: Module 02, Synthesis (prompt engineering).
  • Ethical AI usage: Module 03, Ethics (bias, privacy, adversarial input, transparency, accountability).
  • Gamification: XP, levels, streaks, mastery states and three badges.
  • Progress tracking: a dashboard with completion by module, XP history, a streak calendar, a mastery heatmap and a next-step recommendation.

Design brief

The site looks like a manga page with a tactical interface laid over it: off-white paper, navy-black ink, red used only for emphasis, flat angular panels, hatching instead of soft shadows, and gothic titles. Each module has its own color, and the pictures are built from the lesson content or from your saved progress: the tally marks on the pirate flag count the lessons you have opened and mastered, the islands on the sea chart follow the swarm's layers, and the eye has one ring per vulnerability you inspect. State is always shown by a shape or a word as well as by color. The pictures are drawn in code by a small ink engine, not by hand. The look was inspired by the VALORANT interface, One Piece and Death Note; no artwork, logo, character or typeface from any game or series is used.

Every interactive scene has a text alternative and a keyboard path. Motion respects the reduced-motion setting. The site is a set of static files and runs from any web host.

Your data

There are no accounts and no server. Progress is stored in your browser under one key and is signed so accidental or manual edits are detected. You can export it as a JSON file, import it on another device, or reset it from the Progress page.

How it was built

The process log, planning documents, rubric matrix and compliance notes live in the repository under docs/. They record what the team decided and why.
