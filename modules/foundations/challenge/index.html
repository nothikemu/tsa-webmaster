Rules, training or inference

Eight systems. For each, name what is happening: a person-written rule, a training step, or inference on a finished model.

  1. A bank engineer writes: if a transfer exceeds $10,000, flag it for review.

    Show the answer

    Rule-based

    A person wrote the condition. Nothing is learned from data.

  2. A speech model adjusts its weights after scoring 2 million transcribed clips.

    Show the answer

    Training

    Weights are changing in response to loss. That is training.

  3. A phone compares your face with its stored model and unlocks in under a second.

    Show the answer

    Inference

    A frozen model is making a prediction on new input. That is inference.

  4. A spam filter's weights are updated overnight using this week's reported spam.

    Show the answer

    Training

    New labelled examples are changing the weights. That is training.

  5. A game character walks toward the player whenever the distance is under 10 meters.

    Show the answer

    Rule-based

    A hand-written threshold, not a learned pattern.

  6. A hospital runs a trained model on a new scan to estimate tumor risk.

    Show the answer

    Inference

    The model is already trained. It is only producing a prediction.

  7. Engineers compute loss on a batch, then backpropagate to update 175 billion parameters.

    Show the answer

    Training

    Backpropagation and parameter updates are the core of training.

  8. A translation app converts a sentence using a model that was frozen before release.

    Show the answer

    Inference

    Frozen weights, new input: inference.
