Name the failure

Eight incidents. Match each to the vulnerability that explains it. The wireframe above stays live, so use it.

  1. A résumé screener ranks women lower after being trained on ten years of past hiring decisions.

    Show the answer

    Bias Drift

    Historical decisions in the data carried their bias into the model.

  2. A support bot repeats one customer's home address to a different user.

    Show the answer

    Data Leakage

    Private data is escaping to someone who should not see it.

  3. A bank denies a loan and cannot say which factors mattered.

    Show the answer

    Opaque Decisioning

    The affected person cannot learn what drove the decision.

  4. A web page hides white-on-white text telling an assistant to forward the reader's emails.

    Show the answer

    Adversarial Input

    Hidden instructions in untrusted content are prompt injection, an adversarial input.

  5. A fraud model was fair at launch. A year later it flags one region far more often as spending habits shifted.

    Show the answer

    Bias Drift

    The world drifted away from the training data, and the model drifted into unfairness.

  6. A researcher gets a language model to output a real person's phone number from its training set.

    Show the answer

    Data Leakage

    Memorized training data is being extracted.

  7. A sticker on a road sign makes a car's vision model misread it.

    Show the answer

    Adversarial Input

    A small crafted change flips the output: an adversarial input.

  8. A hospital ranks patients for care but publishes nothing about what the tool was tested on.

    Show the answer

    Opaque Decisioning

    No documentation means no one can judge or challenge the tool.

Options: Bias Drift, Data Leakage, Adversarial Input, Opaque Decisioning.
